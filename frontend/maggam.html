About Maggam Work

Maggam work is a traditional form of embellishment that involves placing mirrors, stones, beads, and crystals on fabric to create stunning visual effects. This intricate art form originated in South India and has been perfected over centuries. Each piece is hand-crafted with precision, creating unique and beautiful designs.
