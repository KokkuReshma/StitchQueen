Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the consultation?

Our initial consultation is typically 30-45 minutes. During this time, we discuss your requirements, show fabric samples, and provide design suggestions.

What should I bring to the consultation?

Bring any inspiration images, color preferences, and a clear idea of what you're looking for. It helps our designers understand your vision better.

How long does the tailoring process take?

The timeline depends on the complexity of the design and current workload. Typically, it takes 2-4 weeks for custom designs and bridal wear.

Can I make changes after booking?

Yes, you can make changes before we start the actual tailoring. We offer unlimited consultations to ensure your satisfaction.

What is your payment policy?

We typically require 50% advance payment to start the work and the remaining balance upon completion. Various payment methods are accepted.

Do you provide alterations?

Yes, we offer free alterations within the first month after delivery. This ensures perfect fit and customer satisfaction.
