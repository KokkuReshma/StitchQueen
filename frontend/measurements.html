Measurement Guide

💡 Tips for Accurate Measurements

  • Wear fitted clothes when measuring
  • Ask someone to help you measure
  • Use a soft measuring tape
  • Keep tape parallel to the ground
  • Don't pull tape too tight
  • Measure twice to confirm

📍 Standard Measurements

  • Bust: Around fullest part of chest
  • Waist: Around narrowest part
  • Hip: Around fullest part of hips
  • Shoulder: From shoulder bone to shoulder bone
  • Length: From shoulder to desired length
  • Sleeve: From shoulder to wrist

🎯 For Best Results

  • Stand straight in front of mirror
  • Don't hold your breath
  • Keep arms relaxed at sides
  • Remove thick clothing
  • Record all measurements clearly
  • Visit us in person for perfect fit
